ABOUT

I began climbing just a few years ago, with the exception of an Ape Man experience as a kid, and quickly found my place within the chalky walls of my local gym. Growing up in the South West meant it took a few years and bit of investigating to find good outdoor bouldering spots, but a handful have made themselves known and there's almost certainly no going back.

Around this time was when I became quite proficient as a cinematographer. My degree had me shooting short films, editing, directing, producing, catering, the lot! Camera work really stuck with me as a medium for communication and creativity. I had always wanted to further my education in linguistics, but cinematography is a language in itself.

Having combined these two aspects of my life, born is an ambition of creating climbing-based films and documentaries. If you see any shots that require a high angle, you know where to find me.
